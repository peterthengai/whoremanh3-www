What Is Hashing?

So you're interested in Hashing. Maybe it's because you've seen strange chalk markings on roads, or flour on trails. Or perhaps you noticed a group of people senselessly running around in kilts. Though most likely, it's because a hasher's silver tongue convinced you to consider trying it out...

Hash House Harriers?

According to Webster:

  • hash house (slang), n., A cheap restaurant.
  • harrier, n., 1. Any of an English breed of dog used for hunting rabbits.
  • 2. A cross-country runner.

According to Us:

Hashing began in Kuala Lumpur and now includes thousands of local chapters, called kennels, around the world. A hare lays a trail in flour or chalk; the pack follows the marks, solves checks and false trails, and reaches the On-In together. Expect trail, songs, jokes, circle, and plenty of “On-On!”—but no official winner. In the shortest version, it is a scavenger hunt with beer and silliness.

Adults age 21 and older of all fitness levels are welcome. Some hashers run hard; plenty of us walk.

Your First Trail

Visiting Utah for a trail? Start with the Trails and Events calendar for current destructions. Regular trails do not require membership or another platform.

  • Check the event listing for the current time and location. The listed time is when everyone gathers. Hares off is about 15 minutes later, and pack off is about 15 minutes after that.
  • Hash cash: Check the event/trail for any specific pricing. Otherwise virgins (first-time hashers) hash free! BYOB is just $1.
  • Drinking is optional. Check the event for what is provided and bring a preferred nonalcoholic drink if needed.
  • Say hello! (Assuming we don’t first.) Don’t be too surprised that no one goes by our real names. Until you yourself are named at an indeterminate time, you will be known as Just <First Name>. e.g. Just John, Just Jane, etc.
  • Chalk talk. Wondering what all those silly marks on the ground are? Worry not. They will be explained to you before we go.
  • Introductions. That’s a lot of people and names to remember. When virgins are present we’ll usually go around the circle before the trail begins and call names so that you might better know the weirdos you’re getting involved with.

Hash Etiquette, Consent, and Privacy

  • Leave the ego at home and be ready to laugh at yourself. Hashing gives adults a few hours to be silly, social, and less serious than everyday life.
  • Our humor is intentionally childish, vulgar, and often sexual. Joining a joke, song, drink, or other activity is always optional.
  • Religion, politics, sexuality, and other controversial topics may become jokes, but humor is never an excuse to target or harass someone.
  • Many hashers keep hashing separate from their professional lives. Use hash names in public posts and ask before sharing anyone’s name or photo.
  • Some hashers run hard and compete informally, but there is no official winner. Checks, false trails, and shortcuts help keep every pace part of the same pack.
  • Keep phone use light on trail so you can stay with the pack. The designated Hash Flash may take photos; ask before photographing or posting anyone who prefers privacy.
  • Don’t point with your finger. We don’t know where that finger’s been. Or worse, we do. Point instead with your elbow.
  • Hashing is an adult activity with vulgar humor and sometimes nudity. None of that implies consent. Clearly communicate your boundaries, respect other people’s boundaries, and ask before touching, photographing, or sharing information about anyone.

Lecture over. Have fun! In fact, fun is mandatory! That’s why we’re all here, and few have more fun than we do!
