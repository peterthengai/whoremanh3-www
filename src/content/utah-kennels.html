Whoreman is the shared name for Utah's Hash House Harriers community. The name appears in surviving records by February 2012, when local hashers organized a road trip to the Betty Ford Rehab Hash, Orange County H3's long-running gathering in Palm Springs. By December 2014, Wasatch H3, Lotsa Damn Shiggy H3, and SL,UT Discovery H3 were collectively known as the Whoreman kennels and were organizing events together.

Today, each kennel maintains its own schedule and traditions, while Whoreman brings the wider community together for campouts, joint events, and Whoreman invasions when travel hashing.

Wasatch H3

Wasatch laid Utah's first recorded trail on July 13, 1991. Founders Mark, Pam & Pwop brought the tradition from Fort Worth H3, and Wasatch became the trunk of the Utah kennel family.

Expect short and long trails, theme events, bar crawls, destination hikes, and plenty of chances not to take yourself too seriously.

Frequency: Every other Saturday.

Lotsa Damn Shiggy H3

Stinky Pinky and Bloody BushDiver laid LDS trail #1 in Layton on July 20, 2011, bringing lineage and traditions from Siete Cerveza, Survivor, and Osan Bulgogi H3s. Expect memorable shiggy, cold beer, and a pack cursing the hares by the finish.

Disclaimer: LDSH3 is in no way associated with any religion, institution, corporation, or political party. Most of our own mothers won't acknowledge us. Yours, however, loves us.

Frequency: Every Thursday.

SL,UT Discovery H3

Improper Cleanse started full-moon discovery trails in early 2013 under LDS's wing; SL,UT became its own kennel that June. It was seeded in the shared shiggy of Wasatch and Lotsa Damn Shiggy and remains proudly Salt Lake Shitty, Utard.

Trails are pick-up style: the Hare Raiser chooses the location and a spin of the Sacred Bottle chooses the hares, including at each new leg of trail.

Frequency: Every full moon.

Salt Lake Old School H3 (SLOSH)

Makes His Own Gravy and Grumpy Sheep F#cker founded SLOSH on September 25, 2016 as a child of Wasatch H3 and a deliberate return to stripped-down, back-to-basics hashing.

Classic 5–7 mile trails, one beer check, and brunch at the finish.

Frequency: Monthly on Sundays.

Fair Weather H3

Shock Cock and One Night Blunder held Fair Weather's first event on January 15, 2017. Hash Prom and other social trails happen whenever the weather, hares, and halfminds align.

Frequency: Whenever scheduled.

Missionary Position BASH

Missionary Position was formally announced on July 28, 2017 as Utah's exclusively bike-hash kennel, building on BASH trails local hashers were already laying. It rides trail on two wheels when weather allows, with occasional snowshoe editions in winter.

Frequency: Monthly when weather allows.
